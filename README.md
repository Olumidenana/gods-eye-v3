## ⚡ What Makes V3 Different (The Zero-Noise Terminal)

Legacy trading dashboards overwhelm you with 40 indicators, confusing candlestick tables, and static rules that take dumb counter-trend trades. **V3 does the exact opposite**:

1. **Self-Explanatory Action Points Only:**
   - Gives you immediate clarity: **BUY / LONG**, **SELL / SHORT**, or **STAND DOWN**.
   - Bold monospace execution targets: Entry (50% CE / FVG), Stop Loss, Breakeven Trigger (+0.78R), Take Profit 1 (+1.25R, 70% Bank), and Take Profit 2 (+3.50R, 30% Macro DOL).
   - Clear, plain-English rationales under **"WHY YOU SHOULD DO THIS"**:
     - *Why this setup?* (Liquidity sweep & institutional displacement in Discount/Premium)
     - *Why this entry & stop?* (Protected below the raid low, entering at FVG Consequent Encroachment)
     - *Why these targets?* (70% profit banked at +1.25R, runner aimed at resting Buy/Sell-side liquidity)
     - *Intermarket SMT Divergence:* Real-time cross-asset verification (XAU/XAG, EUR/GBP).

2. **The Research Lab (Live Backtesting on Real Data):**
   - Directly backtests live historical candle data bar-by-bar.
   - Calculates **Net Realized R-Multiple**, **Win Rate %**, **Profit Factor**, and **Max Drawdown**.
   - Interactive **Equity Curve** visualizing cumulative R growth over time.
   - Complete **Trade Journal Table** with every trade's exact institutional rationale.

3. **Multi-Sensory Notification System:**
   - Web Audio API synthesizer generates pleasant, futuristic audible alerts for Buy, Sell, and Breakeven scratch events without external audio file dependencies.
   - In-app toast banners and browser push alerts when A+ explosive setups trigger.
